# Tips and rules we've worked out

_Things the player asked about, so we don't re-explain them. Keep spoiler-free._

## Game mechanics
- **Weapon Enchantment +1**: +1 to hit and +1 damage.
- **Armour and DEX**: light armour adds full DEX (tooltips don't show it); medium adds max +2; heavy adds none.
- **Off-hand weapons**: only Light weapons, unless you take the Dual Wielder feat. Rapiers and longswords aren't Light.
- **Sneak Attack** (Changuine): no need to be hidden. Triggers with an ally next to the target, with advantage, or via Rakish Audacity (1-on-1 in melee). Needs a Finesse or ranged weapon, which is why the Rupturing Blade beats the Adamantine Longsword.
- **Stealth (C)**: scout with Changuine alone (heavy armour gives Stealth disadvantage), then ambush for Surprise. Hide mid-fight with Cunning Action.
- **"Obscured"** on Dark Justiciar gear = standing in darkness or shadow (half-closed eye icon on the portrait).
- **Luminous Armour**: radiant damage triggers a shockwave; nearby enemies get −1 to attacks. Spirit Guardians with **Radiant** damage combos with it.
- **Great Weapon Master** is a toggle (Passives tab). On with Reckless Attack or against low-AC enemies, otherwise off.
- **Hex for Wyll costs a level 3 pact slot.** Cast once per fight; moving it after a kill is free (bonus action). Budget: Hex + Fireball, then short rest.
- **Concentration**: one at a time (Hypnotic Pattern, Hold Person, Spirit Guardians, Bless, Hex…).
- **Point buy**: 13→14 and 14→15 cost 2 points each.
- **Expertise** only works on skills you're already proficient in.

## Habits
- **Long rest more often.** ~1,600 camp supplies, 40 per rest. Companion and romance scenes happen at camp at night, and traders restock.
- **Selling**: right-click → *Mark as Wares*, then *Sell Wares* in the trade window. Switch party members in the trade window to sell from their bags. **The camp chest can't be sold from** (I'm fairly sure), so keep wares on a party member (Karlach carries the most) or pull them out of the chest before trading.
- **Trade with Astarion or Wyll** (CHA 18). Gifts raise trader attitude, which improves prices.
- No-effect rings, clothes and paintings are just for gold. **Keep orange (story) items**, letters and keys.
- Lockpicking: the player buys kits. No need to plan around it.
- Save in a new slot before big decisions.

## Adamantine Forge: using it again (2026-11-04)
1. **Mould Ejection Lever** first, to clear the old mould.
2. Put the new mould + mithral ore in the centre crucible.
3. **Platform control / main lever** (south edge) → lower the platform. If it was raised all the way, re-lower it with the platform control button.
4. Lava valve → fill the forge.
5. Forge lever (bottom left) → strike. Raise the platform again and collect the item.

## Mods
**Installed via the in-game mod manager (2026-11-04).** They're probably all working; most are subtle:
- Better Inventory UI + Addon: the extra item icons (working).
- Better Topbar / Better Target Info: visual tweaks (top bar; hover an enemy).
- **Camp Event Overhaul** (HyperspaceTowel): invisible. It makes Act 1 camp scenes easier to trigger; nothing shows until you long rest.
- **ContainersExtended** (darkcharl): the auto-sorting bags don't appear mid-game on their own. You need its optional **Vendors** add-on (traders sell the bags) or StartingEquipment/console spawn.
- For visible "!" camp alerts, add **Camp Event Notifications** (Kvalyr) as well.

### Recommended (2026-11-02)
Back up saves first. Add QoL mods mid-run if you like, but don't remove them mid-save.
- Script Extender + Mod Configuration Menu (needed by the rest)
- Camp Event Notifications (don't miss camp scenes)
- Preemptively Label Containers (skip empty containers)
- Auto Send Food / Read Books to Camp
- Carry Weight Increased
- Better Containers (inventory sorting and search)
- Avoid: Party Limit Begone (new saves only), missable-tracker or map-marker mods (spoilers), cheat chests.
