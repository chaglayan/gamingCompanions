## Build notes
**Proficient with:** Light armour. Simple weapons, plus a few martial ones (rapier). Shields and polearms via Human Civil Militia (?).

**Key features:**
- Eldritch Blast + Agonising Blast: his real attack (adds CHA to damage)
- Dark One's Blessing: temp HP when he drops an enemy
- Pact of the Chain: familiar
- Spellcasting: CHA, DC 14, +6 to hit. Pact slots refresh on a short rest.
- Feat: Actor
- Deepened Pact (level 6, Chain): his familiar gets Extra Attack
- Dark One's Own Luck (Fiend 6): once per short rest, add 1d10 to a check or save

**How to play / weapon setup:** Ignore his weapons. Eldritch Blast is better than any of them.
- **[Best now] Hex + Eldritch Blast every turn**: Bonus action: Hex the biggest enemy (+1d6 per beam). Action: Eldritch Blast, two beams of 1d10+4. When the target dies, move Hex for free with your bonus action.
- **[Best now] Fireball for groups**: Only 2 pact slots, but they come back on a short rest. Spend them every fight, then short rest.
- **[Option] Repelling Blast**: Each beam pushes 4.5 m. Grymforge has lava and drops everywhere: push enemies in.
- **[Skip] Rapier / bow**: Never. Eldritch Blast is better.

**Upgrade ideas:**
- Level 5 picks: Fireball (Fiend bonus spell) and Repelling Blast (invocation: Eldritch Blast pushes enemies, great with ledges).
- Anything that adds to Eldritch Blast or raises Charisma or spell DC.
- His robe boosts poison spells: Ray of Sickness and Poison Spray get the +1–4.
- Hat, boots and amulet: any effect at all beats an empty slot.
