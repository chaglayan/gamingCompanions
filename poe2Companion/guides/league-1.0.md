# PoE2 1.0 + Conquerors of Wraeclast

> Pre-launch notes, researched **2026-11-11** (a month before launch). Patch notes are expected ~a week before launch: **refresh this file then.** Anything marked (?) is from pre-launch coverage.

## What's coming (11 Dec 2026, PST)
- **PoE2 leaves Early Access** → version **1.0**, goes **free-to-play** (Steam).
- **Two new classes:**
  - **Shadow**: daggers, **traps**, **stealth**, shadow clones that fight with you. Ascendancies: _unknown_ (not named yet in what I found).
  - **Duelist**: **swords** and **stances**. Ascendancies: **Knight**, **Master-at-Arms** (?).
- **Two new acts (5 and 6)**: completes the 6-act campaign. ~24 new areas, 18 unique bosses, 20+ Champion Rare encounters.
- **New league: Conquerors of Wraeclast**: siege/strategy theme. Recruit troops, build up and siege castles, delegate Atlas progression to your own commanders. Castle treasuries drop a new crafting material that can **reforge gear** (?). Story hook: stop the **Blood Lord** from awakening (?).
- **Endgame / Atlas overhaul** + a broad **balance pass**.
- Launch hour: not announced yet (some sites guess 20:00 GMT).
- Don't mix it up with **PoE1's expansion on 13 Nov 2026**: different game.

## Starter-build shortlist (pre-patch-notes, will change!)
Early-Access-based rankings mention:
- **Disciple of Varashta (Djinn minions)**: easy, comfy summoner.
- **Martial Artist (Hollow Form Whirling Assault)**: called the best melee starter.
- **Gemling Legionnaire (Twister)**.
- **Oracle (Grim Pillars spell totem)**: easy.
- Also strong last league: Spirit Walker, Titan, Deadeye.
- **New classes (Shadow, Duelist)**: no reliable data until patch notes + launch. Fun to try, riskier as a first character.

👉 Final pick: after patch notes, based on the player's preference (see `../journal/overview.md`).
