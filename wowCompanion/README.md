# WoW Companion ⚔️

My World of Warcraft: **Forever** companion. Night Elf Rogue, first time playing WoW. Claude tracks my character, plans talents and gear, and tells me what to do next.

Start with [`journal/overview.md`](journal/overview.md). The rogue guide is in [`guides/rogue.md`](guides/rogue.md).

## How to use it (from the phone)

1. Snap photos of the game screen (list below).
2. Send them to Claude in the chat. Photos aren't kept; Claude writes down what's in them.
3. Ask things like:
   - "I just hit level 12, what do I do?"
   - "Is this item better than mine?" (+ photo of both tooltips)
   - "Where should I level next?"
   - "What does X mean?"

## Photo checklist (most useful first)

| Key | Window | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `C` | Character sheet | Level, stats, gear |
| `N` | Talents | Points spent |
| `P` | Spellbook | Abilities and ranks |
| `L` | Quest log | What you're working on |
| `M` | Map | Where you are |
| `B` | Bags (open all) | Gold, items, space |

Tips: hover an item to show its tooltip before snapping. Shoot straight at the screen, one window per photo.
