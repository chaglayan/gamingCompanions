# Tips & glossary

> Things already explained to the player. Claude: check here before re-explaining.

## Beginner basics
- **Trainer**: new abilities and stronger ranks are bought, not automatic.
- **Hearthstone**: teleports you to the inn you set it at (1h cooldown). Set it at the inn in whatever town you're questing from.
- **Rested XP**: log out in an inn or city and you earn bonus XP for next time (blue XP bar).
- **Eat between fights.** Rogues have no healing spells: food (and bandages) are your heals. Sit down and eat to recover health fast.
- **Sell grey items** (junk) at any vendor. That's your early gold.
- **Bags** are the first good thing to spend gold on.
- **Energy** (yellow bar): rogue resource, refills fast. **Combo points**: dots built by Sinister Strike, spent by finishers like Eviscerate.
- **Stealth** lets you pick fights and skip ones you don't want. It makes you slow, so only use it on tough mobs.
- **Trainer on even levels**: new abilities/ranks appear at 4, 6, 8, 10, 12… (talent point every level from 10).
- **Professions**: Apprentice caps at 75; train Journeyman (from level 10) at the profession trainer to go higher.
- **Camping** (Forever): Cooking lets you place a campfire; other professions add camp buffs (Skinning → Camp Chair, +crit).
- **"Combat build" ≠ only Combat tree**: a build can take points from any tree. The Combat leveling build puts its first 2 points in Assassination (Remorseless Attacks). Talents can be reset at the class trainer for gold.
- **Feeling slow?** Sprint between quest spots, don't stealth-walk to easy mobs, fill your off-hand once Dual Wield is trained, log out in inns for rested XP, Questie to stop searching. Mount at 40.
- **Map highlight on a hill = often a cave underneath.** Look for the entrance around the edge.
- **Gouge → bandage**: Gouge stuns for a few seconds; enough time to use a bandage mid-fight.

## Action bars (explained 2026-11-07)
- More bars: `Esc` → Options → Action Bars → tick the extra bars.
- **Stealth swaps bar 1** to a separate stealth bar. Put openers in the same slots there.
- No need for **Attack** on a bar (any skill or right-click starts auto-attack). Passives can't go on bars.
- Suggested layout:

| Key | Normal bar | Stealth bar |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sinister Strike | Sinister Strike (→ Cheap Shot at 26) |
| 2 | Eviscerate | Pick Pocket |
| 3 | Slice and Dice | Sap |
| 4 | Backstab | Backstab (→ Ambush at 18) |
| 5 | Gouge | Gouge |
| 6 | Kick (12) | Kick |
| 7 | Evasion | Evasion |
| 8 | Sprint | Sprint |
| 9 | Throw | Throw |
| 0 | Food | Food |
| - | Bandage | Bandage |
| = | Stealth | Stealth |

Second bar (click or Shift+key): Shadowmeld, Elune's Light, Hearthstone, Expose Armor (14), Feint (16), Vanish (22), mount (40).

## Questie (addon) — explained 2026-11-08
- Free addon that puts **quest givers (!), turn-ins (?) and objective areas** on your map and minimap. Stops the "where is this?" hunting.
- **Easiest install:** CurseForge app → World of Warcraft → pick the **Forever** version (if missing: "Scan for games" / add the `_forever_` folder) → search Questie → Install.
- **Manual:** download the Forever version → unzip into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`. The `.toc` file must be directly inside `AddOns\Questie\` (not `Questie\Questie\`).
- In game: AddOns button on the character select screen → make sure it's ticked. `/reload` after changes.
- New Forever-only quests may be missing for a while.

- **CurseForge doesn't need to stay open.** It only installs/updates addons; Questie runs inside the game. Open it now and then (e.g. weekly) to click "Update All" — Forever addons update often right now.

## Other addons (recommended 2026-11-08)
Always pick the **Forever** file on CurseForge.
- **Leatrix Plus**: quality-of-life switches. Turn on *auto sell junk*, *auto repair*, *faster auto loot*.
- **Leatrix Maps**: shows **coordinates** on the map (so "41.8, 49.4" makes sense) and removes map fog.
- **Auctionator**: easier Auction House; shows what your leather is worth.
- Later, for dungeons: **Details!** (damage meter) + **Deadly Boss Mods** (boss warnings).
- Skip big UI overhauls for now.

## Rested XP, Zzz, macros, food (explained 2026-11-08)
- **Zzz on your portrait** = you're in a **rested area** (inn/city). Time spent there (best: **logged out**) stores **rested XP**: kills give **double XP** until it's used up. The XP bar turns blue and shows a marker for how far it lasts. Nothing visible happens while sitting; you notice it when killing mobs. People lying down are just using the `/sleep` emote.
- **Macros** (`Esc` → Macros): your own buttons that run several commands at once. Not needed yet; useful later (e.g. Pick Pocket + Ambush in one button at 18).
- **Food clutter**: eat the small stacks first; keep anything that says **"Well Fed"** for tough fights; vendor the rest only if bags are full (it's worth copper).
- **Completed quests**: WoW has no built-in list. Questie's **Journey** window (`/questie` → My Journey) tracks from install; I think it also has a "Quests by Zone" tab.
- **Map coordinates** already show at the bottom-left of the map ("Player: 57.6, 56.7").
- **Green stat numbers** on the character sheet = boosted by a buff right now.

## Leatherworking (explained 2026-11-08)
- **Recipe colours**: orange = always gives a skill point, yellow = usually, green = rarely, grey = never.
- **Ammo pouches / quivers** go in a bag slot but **only hold bullets / arrows**. Rogues throw knives (no ammo) → skip them.
- **LW recipes known (2026-11-08, skill 26):** Light Leather, Handstitched Leather Cloak/Vest/Bracers/Belt/Pants/Boots, Light Armor Kit. Trainer next: **Cured Light Hide at 30** (50c; needs Light Hide + Salt), Light Leather Quiver (skip), Embossed Leather Pants at 50 (2s).
- Recipe costs at the trainer go up slowly with skill (copper → a few silver). Crafting itself only costs **Coarse Thread** (cheap, trade goods vendor) + your own leather.
- Cheapest skill-ups: **Light Armor Kit** (1 Light Leather each). Apply kits to your own gear.
- Better use of leather: **Light Armor Kit** (put it on your own chest/legs/etc. for armor), then **Handstitched Leather Vest / Pants at 25** (wear them), Dark Leather Boots at 35.

## Cities
- **Guards give directions**: talk to any city guard and ask for the bank / trainer / auction house; they put a flag on your map.
- **Bank**: free storage, in every capital. **Auction House**: buy/sell with players.

## Food, water, potions (explained 2026-11-07)
- **Water = mana. Rogues have no mana** (energy only). Sell water.
- **Food**: health refills slowly by itself; eating refills it in ~20–30 sec. Saves waiting between fights. **Cooked "Well Fed" food** also gives stats and, in Forever, an XP buff.
- **Health potions**: vendors don't sell them. Get them from loot, the Auction House (Darnassus), or **First Aid** (Forever lets First Aid craft healing potions). **Bandages** (First Aid, from Linen Cloth) are cheaper and the main rogue heal.
- **Second action bar** has no keys by default: click it, or set keys in Options → Keybindings → Action Bars.

## Gear basics
- **Red text on a tooltip = you can't use it.** Rogues wear **leather** only (no mail/plate), ever. Weapons in red need a weapon skill trained.
- **DPS = average hit ÷ speed.** Speed = seconds between swings. Slow weapons hit harder but less often; fast ones hit softer but more often.
- **Dual wield = any two one-handed weapons** (swords, axes, maces, fist weapons, daggers, mixed). Off-hand does NOT need to be a dagger. Tooltip must say "One-Hand" (not "Main Hand"). Only **Backstab/Ambush** need a dagger, and only in the **main hand**.
- **Selling greens**: low-level greens rarely sell for much on the AH (deposit + 5% cut). Usually just vendor them.
- **Main hand: prefer slow** (Sinister Strike and Riposte use one weapon hit, so a big hit = a big strike). **Off-hand: faster is fine.**
- Stamina = health. Spirit = faster health regen out of combat (minor for rogues). Durability = wear; repair at any vendor with a repair button.
- Compare weapons by **damage per second** on the tooltip. Hold `Shift` while hovering to compare with what you wear.

## Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mob | Any enemy NPC |
| Pull | Start a fight with a mob |
| Aggro | A mob is attacking you |
| Add | An extra mob that joins a fight |
| DPS | Damage per second, or a damage-dealer role (you!) |
| Tank / Healer | The other two group roles |
| Dungeon / Instance | Private group area with bosses (5 players) |
| AH | Auction House: buy/sell with other players (in big cities) |
| Proc | A random effect that triggers ("Riposte procs" = lights up, ready to use) |
| CC | Crowd control: stuns, sap, blind |
| BoP / BoE | Bind on Pickup (yours forever) / Bind on Equip (can sell until you wear it) |
