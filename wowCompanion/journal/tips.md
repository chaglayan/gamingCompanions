# Tips & glossary

> Things already explained to the player. Claude: check here before re-explaining.

## Beginner basics
- **Trainer every level.** New abilities and stronger ranks of old ones. Not automatic in Forever.
- **Hearthstone**: teleports you to the inn you set it at (1h cooldown). Set it at the inn in whatever town you're questing from.
- **Rested XP**: log out in an inn or city and you earn bonus XP for next time (blue XP bar).
- **Eat between fights.** Rogues have no healing spells: food (and bandages) are your heals. Sit down and eat to recover health fast.
- **Sell grey items** (junk) at any vendor. That's your early gold.
- **Bags** are the first good thing to spend gold on.
- **Energy** (yellow bar): rogue resource, refills fast. **Combo points**: dots built by Sinister Strike, spent by finishers like Eviscerate.
- **Stealth** lets you pick fights and skip ones you don't want.

## Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mob | Any enemy NPC |
| Pull | Start a fight with a mob |
| Aggro | A mob is attacking you |
| Add | An extra mob that joins a fight |
| DPS | Damage per second, or a damage-dealer role (you!) |
| Tank / Healer | The other two group roles |
| Dungeon / Instance | Private group area with bosses (5 players) |
| AH | Auction House: buy/sell with other players (in big cities) |
| BoP / BoE | Bind on Pickup (yours forever) / Bind on Equip (can sell until you wear it) |
