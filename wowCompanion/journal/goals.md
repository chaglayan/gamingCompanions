# Goals

## This session (level 9 → 10+)
1. Finish quests around you until **level 10**.
2. At 10: **rogue trainer** (Jannok Breezesong in Dolanaar, I think) → buy everything new.
3. Spend your **first talent point** in Combat (follow the guide).
4. Grab the **level 10 rogue class quest** from the trainer.
5. **Set hearthstone** at the Dolanaar inn if you haven't.
6. Send Claude photos: character sheet (`C`), spellbook (`P`), bags (`B`).

- Pick 2 professions (Skinning + Leatherworking is a solid start).
- Look for a slow sword/axe/mace for your main hand.
